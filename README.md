# vancouver-signals

A dataset and analytical framework tracking neighborhood change in Vancouver through the city's business licence history. The thesis is that certain consumer-facing business types appear before gentrification arrives and certain incumbents disappear as it lands; tracked together across neighborhoods and time, they should reveal where Vancouver is changing now and where it is changing next. Analytical pieces publish to Substack under Bearing Research. See [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) for project shape, conventions, signal taxonomy, and roadmap.
